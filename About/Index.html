Liberative Creative is an independent graphic design practice founded by Michael Liberatore, bringing together creative thinking, visual expression and practical purpose.

With a Bachelor of Communication Design and a Bachelor of Business (Marketing) from Monash University (2018 – 2022), Michael brings a considered approach to design, underpinned by experience across graphic design, eCommerce and digital marketing.

Grounded in a DIY approach with strong commercial awareness, Michael works across branding and identity, editorial, print and digital design to bring campaigns and creative projects to life.

The practice is driven by an interest in culture, independent creative communities, tangibility and DIY culture.

At its core, Liberative Creative is about creating considered, functional and meaningful design - work that extends beyond only aesthetics.
